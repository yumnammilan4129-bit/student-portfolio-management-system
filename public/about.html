INTRODUCTION

What is Student Portfolio Management System?

The Student Portfolio Management System is a web-based application designed to help students maintain their academic and personal development records in an organised manner. Students can enter their information, record skills and achievements, describe projects, and update their portfolio whenever required.

01

Our purpose

Provide students with a convenient place to collect and manage important portfolio information.

02

Our objective

Make student records easier to create, view, search, update and maintain.

03

Our approach

Use a simple interface with frontend validation, backend processing and database storage.

CORE FEATURES

What can the system do?

✓ Create student portfolios with personal and academic details.

✓ Add skills, achievements, certifications and project descriptions.

✓ View, search, edit and delete saved student records.

✓ Submit feedback through a dedicated feedback form.

✓ Validate information in the browser and on the server.
